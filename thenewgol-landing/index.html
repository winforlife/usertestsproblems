— The Story · Christophe Jouret

I sold my company
on a Thursday.

Not because the numbers were bad. They weren't. Not because I was burned out, though I was. I sold it because one morning I was sitting in my car, looking at what I had built, and felt nothing I fully recognized as mine.

We had a services business that worked. Revenue that grew. And this quiet, completely irrational feeling that the whole thing was running me rather than the other way around.

I didn't tell anyone that at the time. You don't, as an entrepreneur. You perform certainty you don't quite feel. You get very good at that performance. Most of us do.

The real conversation, the one that actually matters, almost never happens.

That experience, and everything that came after, is what The New Game of Life is built from. Not a system I learned. A path I walked.

— Step 1 · The Method

Open Your Eyes

The first movement is about seeing. Not analyzing, not solving, just seeing what is actually there, in your patterns, in your reactions, in the space between what you intend and what actually happens.

Most of what shapes us operates below awareness. It is not hidden by design. It simply was never pointed to. This movement creates the conditions for honest, direct seeing, without judgment and without performance.

When you see clearly, everything else becomes possible. The shift in what you observe begins the moment you change the quality of your attention.

— Step 2 · The Method

Orient Your Gaze

The second movement is about understanding. Once you see what is there, the next question is where it comes from. Not to assign blame or build a narrative, but to genuinely understand the origin of the patterns that have been shaping your decisions.

This is where the real shift happens. Not in what you do, but in the place you do it from. When you understand the source, you can choose the direction. You stop reacting and start orienting.

Orienting your gaze means choosing, consciously, where to look next, in your business, in your relationships, in yourself. A different origin produces a different result.

— Step 3 · The Method

Live From There

The third movement is about inhabiting. Not just understanding who you are, but building, deciding, and leading from that place. The work becomes a practice, woven into the daily reality of your company and your life.

This is not a destination. It is a quality of presence — the capacity to act from what is true rather than from what is expected, conditioned, or performed. The character steps aside. What remains is more capable, not less.

Living from there means that coherence is no longer something you work toward. It becomes the ground you operate from. Everything built from that place carries a different quality.
